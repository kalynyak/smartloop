How SmartLoop Works

Three simple steps. Profound insights.

01

Your Watch Does Its Thing

Apple Watch already tracks heart rate, HRV, blood oxygen, sleep stages, workouts, and more. SmartLoop pulls this data via HealthKit — no extra hardware, no battery drain, no setup.

02

You Add the "Why"

Tap the SmartLoop complication anytime to log context. What you ate. How you feel. Symptoms. Stress. Supplements. Quick voice recordings that take seconds but add invaluable context to your health data.

03

AI Connects the Dots

SmartLoop's AI merges sensor data with your voice logs to surface patterns. "Your HRV drops 20% on days you skip breakfast." "Your sleep improves when you walk 8k+ steps." Insights unique to you.
