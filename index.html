How does it know
all of this?

Two channels, always working. Medical-grade sensors track your heart rate, HRV, blood oxygen, sleep, and stress around the clock. The always-on microphone captures what's happening in your life — what you ate, how you're feeling, that meeting that went sideways. You choose how much to share: log moments when you want, or turn on continuous listening. When you talk to ChatGPT, Gemini, or Grok through SmartLoop, they understand your full context. That's the difference between a chatbot and a personal assistant who truly knows you.

Your Privacy Is Non-Negotiable.

We built SmartLoop with privacy at its core. Your conversations and health data are yours — period.

shield

Official AI APIs

We use official APIs from OpenAI (ChatGPT), Google (Gemini), and xAI (Grok). These APIs are designed for enterprise use and come with strict data protection guarantees.

model_training

Never Used for Training

Your conversations are never used to train AI models. Official API terms explicitly prohibit using your data for model improvement. Your words stay your words.

delete_forever

No Data Stored by AI

AI providers do not store your conversations or voice data. Requests are processed in real-time and discarded. No logs, no history on their servers.

lock

Your Data, Your Device

Health data and voice logs stay on your device and in your private iCloud. We never sell, share, or monetize your personal information. Full control is always yours.
